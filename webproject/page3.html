Resources for parents

Choosing what comes after GCSEs is a big decision. Here are the essentials to help you understand T Levels and start a useful conversation.

What is a T Level?

A T Level is a two-year technical qualification combining classroom study with an industry placement. It is worth three A Levels and can lead to skilled work or further study.

Questions to ask

Talk about the subjects your young person enjoys, the practical skills they want to build and the kind of placement they would find exciting.
